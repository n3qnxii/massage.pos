v55:
- Reworked Next Turn into equal-turn rounds.
- Every therapist receives one turn per round before the rotation starts again.
- Booking and Request treatments count as that therapist's turn, even when that therapist was not the current Next Turn.
- Example A/B/C: if B receives a booking first, B is counted; the next normal turns are A then C. After all three have one turn, a new round begins from A.
- Walk-in, Booking and Request therefore share the same fair turn ledger without forcing booking/request customers onto another therapist.
